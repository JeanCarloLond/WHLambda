# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. By participating, you are expected to uphold it.

In short: be welcoming, be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, give and gracefully accept
constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people who use WaveHeaven. Harassment, insults and
personal or political attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/JeanCarloLond/WHLambda/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even for conduct issues) or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. Enforcement follows the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
